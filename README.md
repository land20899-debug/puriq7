# Andrei Chikatilo — short animated history

Open `index.html` in a browser to watch the short animated video.

It is a 90-second documentary-style animated sequence covering:
- Chikatilo's early life
- his social isolation and troubled development
- the pattern of murders in the Soviet era
- the investigation and failures of the system
- his arrest, trial, and legacy

No external dependencies are required.

Quick start:
1. Open `index.html` in a web browser.
2. Click Play to start the animation.
3. Use Restart to replay the sequence.

This is designed as a brief, respectful, and non-graphic historical retelling.

Repository status:
- HTML/CSS/JS animated mini-project
- Browsable animation, under 2 minutes
- Intended for educational and historical presentation
